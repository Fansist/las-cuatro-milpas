The short menu

It has barely changed in decades. Order at the counter, then eat in or take it with you.

  • Flour tortillas

    Handmade and rolled fresh daily. By the dozen to take home, flour or corn.

  • Tamales

    Pork, filled by hand in the kitchen. By the piece or by the dozen.

  • Rolled tacos

    Taquitos.

  • Rice and beans

    Swimming in a savory broth.

  • Chorizo con huevo

    Chorizo and eggs.

  • Burritos

  • Menudo

    Saturdays only.

Prices are on the board at the counter.

From Logan Ave to National Ave

One of San Diego's oldest restaurants, run by four generations of one family.

  1. 1933

    1857 Logan Ave

    The original counter, for more than ninety years. Food came over the counter to wooden benches, and at lunch the line ran down the block.

  2. 2026

    1985 National Ave, Suite 1131

    Reopened on May 12, inside Mercado del Barrio, with the same short menu.
